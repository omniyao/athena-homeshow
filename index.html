Guess the Bubbles in the Bath – Terms & Conditions

1. Promoter: The promoter is OPR Limited, the Home of Athena & Clearlite Bathrooms (“Athena” or “the Promoter”).


2. Eligibility: Entry is open to New Zealand residents aged 18 years or over. Employees of OPR Limited and their immediate families, and any other persons directly involved in the administration of the competition, are not eligible to enter.


3. How to Enter: To enter, participants must complete the official Guess the Bubbles in the Bath entry form during the applicable competition period, provide required contact details, submit their numeric bubble guess, and nominate one eligible Athena product displayed on the stand. No purchase necessary.


4. Eligible Prizes: Entrants may select one prize from the Athena products specifically identified as eligible on the stand. The product selected at the time of entry cannot be changed after the competition closes.


5. One Entry Per Person: Only one Guess the Bubbles entry per person per Home Show is permitted. Participation in both Guess the Bubbles and the separate Array Design Studio activation is permitted.


6. Competition Periods:
• Auckland Home Show: Opens 10 Sept 2026, closes 6pm on 13 Sept 2026. Winner drawn 14 Sept 2026.
• Christchurch Home Show: Opens 16 Oct 2026, closes 6pm on 18 Oct 2026. Winner drawn 19 Oct 2026.


7. How the Winner is Determined: The entrant whose guess is closest to the actual number of bubbles in the bath wins. If two or more entrants submit equally close winning guesses, the winner will be selected by random draw from those tied entries.


8. The Prize: The winner receives the nominated Athena product only. Installation, plumbing, and building work are not included. Prize is non-transferable and not redeemable for cash.


9. Winner Notification: The winner will be contacted via email/phone and must respond within five (5) business days.


10. Marketing Consent: Marketing consent is optional and unticked by default. Choosing not to receive marketing communications will not affect an entrant’s chance of winning.
